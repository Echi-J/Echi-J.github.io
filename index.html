HOME ABOUT PROJECT GROUP WORK

I come from a background in landscape architecture and arts management and cultural policy. My interests have always been closely connected to nature, from landscape design and botanical illustration to my recent experience working with plants. I’m now interested in exploring how technology can help us understand and interact with the environments around us.

I joined Connected Environments to develop my technical skills and explore IoT, environmental sensing, data visualisation, and real-time environmental data. I’m particularly curious about what happens when physical environments, people, and digital systems are connected, and how these connections can create new ways of understanding our surroundings.

Outside UCL, I enjoy botanical drawing, handcrafts, playing guqin, hiking, and exploring plants and natural landscapes. I also enjoy thinking through complex problems and finding connections between seemingly unrelated fields.
